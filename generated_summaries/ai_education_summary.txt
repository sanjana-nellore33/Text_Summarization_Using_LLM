Artificial Intelligence is revolutionizing education by enhancing personalized learning and accessibility. AI-driven tools enable students to grasp complex concepts, obtain immediate feedback, and progress at individual rates. Meanwhile, educators can utilize AI to pinpoint students requiring extra assistance and refine instructional techniques. 

Despite these benefits, integrating AI into education presents significant challenges, including data privacy concerns, excessive reliance on technology, and the risk of inaccurate information. Consequently, the document concludes that AI must function as a supportive tool for teachers and students rather than a replacement for human interaction.
